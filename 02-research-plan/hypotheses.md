# Hypotheses

H1: Higher comorbidity burden (diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol) is associated with worse balance performance (shorter failure time and higher odds of failing balance conditions).

H2: Lower dietary micronutrient intake (vitamin B12 and folate) is associated with worse balance performance. **Note: Vitamin D is unavailable in cycle C and omitted.**

H3: Comorbidity burden modifies micronutrient associations such that lower vitamin B12 and folate intake is more strongly associated with poor balance among adults with higher condition counts.
